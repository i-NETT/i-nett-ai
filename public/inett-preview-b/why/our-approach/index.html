Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

What does a strategic business review cover?

Your business plan for the next 6 to 36 months, the technology decisions it depends on, and what each will cost, in business language rather than ticket counts.

How is this different from a typical MSP?

A typical MSP is a technical support vendor measured on uptime. We work as business advisors first, so technology decisions follow the business plan and are measured on outcomes.

What is the initial term?

Most clients have a three to six month initial term, then month-to-month. If we are not doing our job, stop paying us.

Do we lose the local touch with a national alliance behind you?

No. Your day-to-day team is local and knows your people by name; the alliance adds hundreds of certified US-based technicians and purchasing power across 158 marketplaces.

Will you work with our in-house IT?

Yes. We add strategic guidance, specialized expertise and overflow support around your existing team.
