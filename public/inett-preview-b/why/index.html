Frequently Asked Questions

How does the month-to-month model work?

Unlike traditional MSPs that lock you into multi-year agreements, we ask for a short initial term to prove ourselves. After that, we operate on a month-to-month partnership model. This means we must continually demonstrate value to earn your business each month — no multi-year commitment, no being trapped in a long-term contract.

What makes your cyber insurance advantage different?

As a Lloyd's of London cyber insurance partner, our highly rated managed security helps the firms we manage qualify for coverage with fewer of the common escape clauses that leave typical policies virtually worthless. Most policies contain technical requirements that, if not met, void coverage when you need it most. Our program helps ensure true protection, often at discounted pricing.

Can you support our existing IT team?

Absolutely. Many of our clients have internal IT resources. We collaborate effectively with your team, providing specialized expertise, strategic guidance, and supplemental support that complements your existing capabilities.

What size businesses do you typically work with?

We specialize in serving mid-market businesses with 25-100 employees across Southern California, Arizona, and Nevada. Our solutions are particularly valuable for organizations where technology directly impacts revenue and competitive position.

What industries do you specialize in?

We have extensive experience across multiple industries, with particular expertise in professional services, healthcare, manufacturing, and non-profit organizations. Our approach is adaptable to the unique requirements of various sectors while maintaining consistent business-focused outcomes.
