Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

How does the month-to-month model work?

We ask for a short initial term, usually three to six months, to prove ourselves. After that we are month-to-month, so we earn your business every month or you stop paying us.

What makes your cyber insurance advantage different?

Our partner writes cyber insurance and nothing else, placed with Lloyd's of London. Because our managed security is rated highly for reducing risk, firms we manage can qualify for stronger coverage with fewer escape clauses, often at discounted pricing.

Can you support our existing IT team?

Yes. Many of our clients have internal IT, and we add specialized expertise, strategic guidance and overflow support around them.

What size businesses do you typically work with?

Mid-market businesses with 25 or more employees across San Diego, Orange and Los Angeles counties, plus Arizona, Nevada and Washington, especially where technology directly affects revenue and competitive position.

What industries do you specialize in?

Professional services, healthcare, manufacturing and non-profit organizations, with the same business-first approach applied to each.
