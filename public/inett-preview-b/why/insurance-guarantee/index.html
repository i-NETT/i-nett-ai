Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from standard cyber insurance?

Our partner writes cyber insurance and nothing else, placed with Lloyd's of London. Because our managed security is rated highly for reducing risk, firms we manage can qualify for stronger coverage with fewer of the escape clauses that undo typical policies at claim time.

Will this cost more than our current policy?

Often not. Firms we manage can qualify for discounted pricing, and some see further reductions in later policy years as their security posture is reassessed.

Do we need specific security controls to qualify?

Yes. Carriers now demand proof of controls such as MFA, tested backups and endpoint protection, and we deploy and document those controls as part of managed security, so what you attest to is what you actually run.

What happens at claim time?

Carriers audit the controls you attested to. Because we manage and document those controls every month, the evidence is ready, and we coordinate with your broker and the insurance partner on the response.

Does coverage adapt as we grow?

Yes. Coverage is reviewed as your business and technology change, and the security section is updated with your broker at renewal.
