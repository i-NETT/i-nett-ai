From First Conversation to Trusted Partner

How We Evaluate and Onboard Every Client

We move deliberately, on purpose. Rushing to "optimize" an environment we don't understand yet is how MSPs break things.

1

Discovery Call

A free, no-obligation conversation about your business goals, current pain points, and what "working" actually looks like for your team — not a sales pitch about our tools.

2

Technology & Risk Assessment

A full audit of your existing systems, security posture, and vulnerabilities. We look at what's actually running in your environment, not just what's on paper.

3

Custom Proposal & Fit Check

We build a plan around your specific risk profile, growth goals, and budget — and we tell you honestly if we're not the right fit for each other.

4

Onboarding & Documentation

Before we change anything, we learn everything: full asset inventory, network documentation, credential collection, and a baseline security review.

The 90-Day Stabilization Window

We Don't Touch Your Environment for the First 90 Days — Unless It's an Emergency or a Real Risk

Once onboarding documentation is complete, we hold a 90-day stabilization period before making any changes to your environment. We observe, document, and understand the reasons behind how your systems are configured before we touch anything — the only exceptions are active emergencies or issues that pose a genuine risk to your organization, which we address immediately regardless of where you are in the stabilization window.

After 90 days, we move into full ongoing management: the Manage, Analyze, and Optimize discipline above, quarterly business reviews, and the methodology detailed on Our Approach.

What it costs to move up

Your rate does not climb as your maturity does

Getting a client from level one to level five is our job, not a surcharge. The work of moving you up the journey is included in what you already pay.

What stays the same

  • Moving from maturity level 1 to level 5 does not change your rate
  • Unlimited help desk, remote support and on-site support on every plan
  • No per-ticket charges, no call-out fees, no charge for a site visit
  • Every support call is a cost to us, which is why we work to make them rarer
  • Because the core infrastructure is already paid for, each person you add costs less than the last

The few things that can change it

  • Hardware genuinely at end of life, or that no longer meets current security standards, such as a firewall that cannot support what your policy now requires
  • Infrastructure your organization has simply outgrown, where the existing kit can no longer carry the load
  • New users and new devices, charged at the same per-unit rate you already have

We would rather you read this here than discover it in an invoice. In every case you see the cost and the reasoning before anything is agreed.
