The 90-day stabilization window

We do not touch your environment for the first 90 days, unless it is an emergency or a real risk.

  • No changes for the first 90 days after documentation is complete
  • We observe, document and learn why systems are set up as they are
  • Exceptions: active emergencies and genuine risks, fixed immediately
  • After 90 days: full ongoing management and quarterly business reviews

What it costs to move up

Your rate does not climb as your maturity does.

Getting a client from level one to level five is our job, not a surcharge.

What stays the same

  • Moving from maturity level 1 to level 5 does not change your rate
  • Unlimited help desk, remote and on-site support on every plan
  • No per-ticket charges, no call-out fees, no charge for a site visit
  • Every support call is a cost to us, so we work to make them rarer
  • Core infrastructure already paid for, so each person you add costs less than the last

The few things that can change it

  • Hardware genuinely at end of life or below current security standards
  • Infrastructure your organization has simply outgrown
  • New users and devices, at the same per-unit rate you already have

We would rather you read this here than find it in an invoice: you see the cost and the reasoning before anything is agreed.

Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

What happens in the first 90 days?

We document everything and change nothing unless it is an active emergency or a genuine risk. After 90 days we move into full ongoing management with quarterly business reviews.

Does our monthly rate go up as you improve our environment?

No. Moving from maturity level one to level five is our job, not a surcharge, and unlimited help desk, remote and on-site support are on every plan. The only changes are hardware at end of life, infrastructure you have outgrown, and new users or devices at the same per-unit rate.

What certifications does your team hold?

Tier 1 Microsoft Partner, Microsoft Azure Solutions Architect, Azure Administrator Associate and Microsoft 365 Identity and Services, and the entire team is HIPAA compliant.

How do you use AI in your own support?

We analyze our own support history so engineers get faster, more accurate resolution suggestions on every ticket, governed the same way we govern AI for clients.
