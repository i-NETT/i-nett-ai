New Venture Escrow: From Technical Chaos to Cloud-Powered Agility

The Challenge

New Venture Escrow was previously working with a top-rated managed service provider yet faced critical system failures that threatened their operations. With an excessive 11 servers supporting just 47 employees, they experienced a catastrophic situation when 6 servers simultaneously failed. Their CFO was forced to manually schedule employee system access in limited time windows, asking staff to accomplish 8-hour workloads in just 2-hour shifts.

Solution for New Venture Escrow

i-NETT executed a rapid, strategic migration to the cloud. Our emergency response included:

  • Same-day data migration to secure cloud infrastructure
  • Creation of fully operational business environment within 24 hours
  • Systematic testing with management team by Saturday afternoon
  • Complete system testing with full staff by Sunday
  • Seamless transition with minimal on-site setup by Monday morning

Results:

We reduced their server count from 11 down to 4 and completely transformed their infrastructure. Now they're not only more efficient—they're truly agile. If they need to relocate offices, they simply move their firewall, plug it in, and continue operations without disruption.

Additionally, i-NETT identified critical gaps in their cybersecurity insurance that left funds vulnerable—issues their previous provider never addressed.

Anchor Audio: Enterprise-Grade Security for Mission-Critical Systems

The Challenge

Anchor Audio had been with a major IT services firm for 10 years. While initially satisfactory, service quality deteriorated significantly in recent years. Support became so unreliable that their technically-inclined team stopped seeking help and wasted valuable engineering time troubleshooting IT issues themselves. A network assessment revealed shocking security vulnerabilities—their Sage accounting server had been infiltrated 571 times in just 24 hours and thousands of times in a week due to improperly configured security.

Solution for Anchor Audio

i-NETT's comprehensive assessment identified critical security gaps and system inefficiencies. Our solution included:

  • Strategic firewall reconfiguration to close all unnecessary ports
  • Implementation of enterprise-grade protection for their financial systems
  • Custom security protocols tailored to their specific business operations
  • Development of a strategic roadmap for ongoing system optimization

Results:

With properly secured systems and significantly improved performance, Anchor Audio's engineering team now focuses on product development rather than IT troubleshooting. Their critical financial systems are protected from intrusions, providing leadership with peace of mind and allowing the company to focus on growth rather than system vulnerabilities.
