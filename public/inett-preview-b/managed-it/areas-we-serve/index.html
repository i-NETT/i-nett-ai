Questions about coverage

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cover directly?

San Diego, Orange, Los Angeles, Ventura and Riverside counties and the Central Coast from our California offices, plus Arizona and Nevada through technicians who live in those metros.

How does coverage outside those regions work?

Through our Technology Assurance Group membership, on-site technicians reach most of the United States within four business hours, and parts of Canada, while you keep one accountable contact.

Do you have an office in every city listed?

No. Our offices are in San Diego and Ventura. The list shows where we work on-site and remotely, and a city only gets its own page when there is something true to say about it alone.

What if our city is not on the list?

Ask anyway. Between our own teams and the alliance we reach most of the country, and we will tell you plainly if you are somewhere we cannot serve well.
