Questions Arizona leaders ask

Frequently asked questions

Do you have an office in Phoenix?

Our offices are in San Diego and Ventura. Arizona is covered by technicians who live in the Phoenix metro and drive to client sites, so on-site help is already in the state.

Which parts of Arizona do you cover?

Maricopa County including Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler and Gilbert, plus Pinal County, Pima County including Tucson, and Yavapai County including Prescott and Sedona.

Is the service the same as your California clients get?

Yes. Unlimited remote and on-site support, 24/7/365 monitoring, enterprise-grade platforms and the same month-to-month terms.

What about our locations outside Arizona?

Our Technology Assurance Group membership puts on-site technicians within reach across most of the United States, so you keep one accountable contact instead of a provider in every state.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Month-to-month after your initial term, and if we are not doing our job, stop paying us.
