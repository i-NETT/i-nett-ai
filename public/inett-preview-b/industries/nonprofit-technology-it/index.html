Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

How does the Microsoft grant program work, and can we qualify?

Microsoft gives eligible non-profits up to $2,000 a year in Azure credits, plus non-profit pricing on Microsoft 365 and security tools. We check eligibility, complete the application with you, plan how to use what you receive, and renew the grant every year.

What is a technology fund?

A dedicated reserve for technology, built from grants, donors and a small planned contribution, with a refresh cycle the board can see years ahead. Upgrades become planned line items instead of emergencies.

How do you keep technology costs down without cutting quality?

Grants and donations first, existing systems optimized before anything is replaced, and a technology fund so upgrades are planned rather than emergencies.

Can you help us show technology results to the board and donors?

Yes. We report how each investment changed program delivery and operating cost, in language a board can use in a funding conversation.

How do you protect donor, client and volunteer data?

Role-based access, encryption for sensitive records, MFA for staff and volunteers, and offboarding the day someone leaves.

Can you support multiple locations and remote workers?

Yes. Cloud-based systems keep every location and remote volunteer on the same secure environment, with unlimited on-site and remote support on every plan.
