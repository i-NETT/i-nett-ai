Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Microsoft grant program work, and how can we qualify?

Microsoft provides eligible non-profits with up to $2,500 in credits for cloud services. Our team helps you navigate the qualification process, complete the application, and implement a strategic plan to maximize these credits. We've helped dozens of non-profits successfully access these grants and can guide you through each step of the process.

How do you help non-profits manage technology costs while maintaining quality?

Our approach combines several strategies: leveraging available grants and donations, implementing scalable solutions that grow with your needs, optimizing existing systems rather than replacing them when possible, and creating technology funds that provide sustainable resources for ongoing needs. This comprehensive approach ensures you get enterprise-grade capabilities within your budget constraints.

Can you help us demonstrate technology ROI to our board and donors?

Yes. We understand that non-profit technology investments must demonstrate clear mission impact. We help you create metrics and reporting frameworks that show how technology investments translate into improved program delivery, operational efficiency, and mission advancement. These frameworks help justify technology expenses to boards and donors who may otherwise view technology as a purely administrative cost.

How do you address the unique data security needs of non-profits?

Non-profits often manage sensitive donor, client, and volunteer information across multiple systems. Our security approach focuses on protecting this data without creating barriers to collaboration. We implement role-based access controls, encryption for sensitive information, secure remote access, and comprehensive data protection policies—all designed specifically for non-profit operational models.

Can you support our organization if we have multiple locations or remote workers?

Absolutely. We specialize in creating secure, accessible technology environments that support distributed teams and multiple locations. Our cloud-based solutions enable collaboration across locations while maintaining security, and our standardized approach ensures consistent technology experience regardless of where your team members work.
