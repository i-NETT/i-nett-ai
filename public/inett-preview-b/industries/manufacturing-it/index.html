Client Success Story

Anchor Audio

The Challenge

Anchor Audio had been with a major IT services firm for 10 years. While initially satisfactory, service quality deteriorated significantly in recent years. Support became so unreliable that their technically-inclined team stopped seeking help and wasted valuable engineering time troubleshooting IT issues themselves. A network assessment revealed shocking security vulnerabilities—their Sage accounting server had been infiltrated 571 times in just 24 hours and thousands of times in a week due to improperly configured security.

Solution for Anchor Audio

i-NETT's comprehensive assessment identified critical security gaps and system inefficiencies. Our solution included:

  • Strategic firewall reconfiguration to close all unnecessary ports
  • Implementation of enterprise-grade protection for their financial systems
  • Custom security protocols tailored to their specific business operations
  • Development of a strategic roadmap for ongoing system optimization

Results

With properly secured systems and significantly improved performance, Anchor Audio's engineering team now focuses on product development rather than IT troubleshooting. Their critical financial systems are protected from intrusions, providing leadership with peace of mind and allowing the company to focus on growth rather than system vulnerabilities.

We implement specialized security frameworks designed specifically for manufacturing environments, creating secure zones that allow data flow between operational technology and business systems while preventing threats from crossing between environments. Our approach protects both production systems and business information without compromising operational efficiency.

Yes. We have extensive experience integrating with manufacturing systems from all major vendors, including SCADA systems, PLCs, MES platforms, and ERP solutions.
