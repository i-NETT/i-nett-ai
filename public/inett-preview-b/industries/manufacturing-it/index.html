Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

How do you secure a plant that mixes OT and IT?

We build secure zones so data flows between production and business systems while threats cannot cross, and we schedule every change around your shifts.

Can you integrate with our existing manufacturing systems?

Yes. We work with SCADA, PLCs, MES and ERP from the major vendors, starting with what you already own rather than replacing it.

How can we use operational data for better decisions?

We collect data across the production environment and turn it into dashboards leadership actually reads, so efficiency, quality and margin decisions are made on numbers.

What makes your manufacturing IT different from a general provider?

We understand production processes and operational technology, so solutions fit the floor instead of creating friction on it.

How fast do you respond to a production-critical issue?

Monitoring runs 24/7/365 and production-critical systems follow emergency protocols, so an engineer who knows your plant is on it within minutes.
