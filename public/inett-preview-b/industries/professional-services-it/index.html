Client Success Story

New Venture Escrow

The Challenge

While not a manufacturing company, New Venture Escrow faced infrastructure challenges similar to those encountered in modern manufacturing environments. They were previously working with a top-rated managed service provider yet faced critical system failures that threatened their operations. With an excessive 11 servers supporting just 47 employees, they experienced a catastrophic situation when 6 servers simultaneously failed. Their CFO was forced to manually schedule employee system access in limited time windows, asking staff to accomplish 8-hour workloads in just 2-hour shifts.

Solution for New Venture Escrow

i-NETT executed a rapid, strategic migration to the cloud. Our emergency response included:

  • Same-day data migration to secure cloud infrastructure
  • Creation of fully operational business environment within 24 hours
  • Systematic testing with management team by Saturday afternoon
  • Complete system testing with full staff by Sunday
  • Seamless transition with minimal on-site setup by Monday morning

Results

We reduced their server count from 11 down to 4 and completely transformed their infrastructure. Now they're not only more efficient—they're truly agile. If they need to relocate offices, they simply move their firewall, plug it in, and continue operations without disruption.

Additionally, i-NETT identified critical gaps in their cybersecurity insurance that left funds vulnerable—issues their previous provider never addressed.
