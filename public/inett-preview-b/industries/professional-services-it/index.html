Frequently asked questions

How do you protect client information?

Client files are encrypted, every login uses MFA, access is granted by matter or engagement and every control is logged, so you can show a client, a regulator or a carrier exactly what is in place.

How is your approach different from a traditional IT provider?

Client confidentiality is the product your firm sells, so we treat it as the product, not a checkbox, and fit the technology to how the firm earns.

Can you support a firm with multiple offices?

Yes. Through the Technology Assurance Group alliance we deliver the same service and security standard across 158 marketplaces in the US and Canada.

Can you work with our case management, tax or financial software?

Yes. We integrate and support the specialized applications your firm already runs rather than replacing them.

Our staff already use public AI tools. What should we do?

Govern it rather than ban it. Fortify AI gives the firm every leading model in one secure login with zero data retention, so a memo or a contract never leaves your walls.
