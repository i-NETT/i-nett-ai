Client Success Story

Active Care Living

The Challenge

Active Care Living, operating nursing homes and assisted living facilities across multiple locations, struggled with IT providers who couldn't meet their specialized healthcare industry needs. Their original provider of 20 years couldn't adapt to evolving cybersecurity requirements from insurance companies. Their next provider created more problems than solutions, breaking critical systems including their financial reporting website and cross-location employee database.

Solution for Active Care Living

i-NETT delivered comprehensive solutions built around understanding their unique healthcare operations:

  • Fixed mission-critical systems within two weeks (systems their previous provider failed to repair after a full year of attempts)
  • Restored vital cross-location employee databases essential for regulatory compliance
  • Implemented secure, efficient shared folder systems for financial reporting and regulatory audits
  • Developed specialized systems to manage resident trust funds that require quarterly auditing
  • Created technical solutions enabling residents to connect with families during COVID restrictions

Results

The streamlined systems have simplified regulatory compliance, improved communication between facilities, and reduced administrative overhead. Most importantly, their focus on customer service and business understanding has eliminated IT-related stress for leadership.
