Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

How do you handle HIPAA for a practice or care facility?

We assess risk against HIPAA, write policies for how you actually operate, train every employee and instrument the controls so proof is available on demand. Our entire team is HIPAA-compliant.

Can you work with our existing EHR?

Yes. We integrate and optimize the EHR you already run, with imaging, lab, pharmacy, scheduling and billing, rather than replacing it.

Do you support telemedicine and remote care?

Yes. Secure video consultation, remote monitoring and virtual care workflows are built to meet HIPAA requirements.

What makes your healthcare IT different from a general provider?

We understand clinical workflows, medical terminology and the 24/7 nature of care, so solutions fit the environment instead of creating friction in it.

How fast do you respond when a clinical system goes down?

Monitoring runs 24/7/365 and critical clinical systems follow emergency protocols, so an engineer who knows your environment is on it within minutes.
