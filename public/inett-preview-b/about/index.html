Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

How long has i-NETT been in business?

The company was founded in Ventura in 1982 as Southern Counties Telephone, rebranded in 1988, and became i-NETT in 2021.

Where are your offices?

Headquarters is at 9655 Granite Ridge Drive, Suite 200, San Diego, CA 92123, with teams in Ventura, Phoenix and Las Vegas.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Most clients have a 3 to 6 month initial term, then the relationship is month-to-month. If we are not doing our job, stop paying us.

What certifications does your team hold?

Tier 1 Microsoft Partner, Azure Solutions Architect, Azure Administrator Associate and M365 Identity and Services, and the entire team is HIPAA compliant.
