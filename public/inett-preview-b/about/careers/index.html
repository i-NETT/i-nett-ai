Transform Technology Into Competitive Advantage

Join Our Team of Business-First Technology Revolutionaries

Explore exciting technology careers with i-NETT, a leading provider of managed services and IT solutions. We offer rewarding IT jobs in Southern California, Arizona, and Nevada for professionals who want to transform how businesses leverage technology.

With 25+ years of experience — and two brand reinventions behind us to prove we stay current — i-NETT has revolutionized how Southern California, Arizona, and Nevada businesses leverage technology. As a strategic technology partner, we deliver enterprise-grade IT solutions with deeply personal service that transforms technology from a business expense into a measurable competitive advantage.

Where Business Advisors Meet Technology Experts

At i-NETT, we're not just technicians – we're strategic partners who understand both business and technology. Our approach combines the wisdom of trusted advisors with the boldness of industry disruptors:

  • Month-to-month accountability – We earn your business through results, not contracts
  • Enterprise-level resources – National reach spanning 150 cities with local, personalized support
  • Cyber insurance advantage – Lloyd's of London cybersecurity insurance partnership
  • Business-first thinking – Strategic solutions that drive profitability and competitive advantage

Our Core Values

Our team members are our most valued assets, essential participants with a shared responsibility in fulfilling our mission:

  • Team member involvement – We promote open communication, teamwork, and cooperation
  • Customer-first approach – We put our customers first and live our brand
  • Innovation mindset – We embrace innovation and calculated risk-taking to drive growth
  • Problem-solving excellence – We identify and solve problems with a focus on results
  • Daily impact – Our actions make the difference every day

Our policies and practices are built on dedication to assisting every employee in reaching their full potential in both performance and reward.
