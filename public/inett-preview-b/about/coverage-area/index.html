Frequently asked questions

Do you send technicians on site?

Yes. Unlimited on-site and remote support is included on every plan, from our own teams in Southern California, Arizona and Nevada.

We have locations outside the Southwest. Can you cover them?

Through our Technology Assurance Group membership, on-site technicians are within reach across most of the United States within four business hours, and into parts of Canada, with i-NETT as your single point of contact.

Is Phoenix or Las Vegas served from California?

No. Arizona is covered by technicians who live in the Phoenix metro, and Nevada service delivery is led from Las Vegas by our Vice President of Operations.
