Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

When was i-NETT founded?

In 1982, in Ventura, California, as Southern Counties Telephone, Inc. It rebranded in 1988 and became i-NETT in 2021.

Why rebrand twice?

Each rebrand marked a real change in the work, from cabling and phone systems to fully managed IT, cloud and security. We would rather rebuild the company than coast on a name.

What did the alliance change?

Dale Stein co-founded the Technology Assurance Group in 1998. In 2009 he became a partner and CEO of i-NETT, then Voice Smart Networks, and brought the company into the alliance: 158 marketplaces across North America, shared business practices, benchmarking and manufacturer partnerships a regional provider could not reach alone.
