Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 17, 2026

i-nett.com ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website i-nett.com, use our services (including any text messaging services), or otherwise interact with us. By using our site or services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, postal address (if you voluntarily provide it via forms, contact requests, or SMS opt-in).

Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and similar information collected via cookies or analytics tools.

Message Data: If you opt in to receive text messages from us, we collect your mobile phone number, opt-in confirmation, and message history with us.

Other: Any additional information you voluntarily submit (e.g., comments, uploads).

We do not collect sensitive personal information (e.g., health or financial data) unless explicitly required and disclosed.

2. SMS opt-in details

  • By providing your phone number to i-nett and consenting to our SMS Opt-In, you consent to receive SMS messages from i-nett for updates, notifications, and other important information related to your services.
  • You can opt-out of SMS messages at any time by contacting us at service@i-nett.com. Or by replying with the word "STOP" to any SMS message you receive from i-nett.
  • You must be 18 years of age or older to opt-in to our SMS Opt-In.
  • Messaging and data rates may apply. Check with your carrier for details.
  • You are responsible for any data, messaging, or fees charged by your carrier for SMS messages.
  • Carrier liability disclaimer: Your carrier is not responsible for any messages sent or received by i-nett.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: Provide and improve our services, including sending text messages you have opted into. Respond to inquiries and provide customer support. Send administrative, promotional, or marketing communications (only with your consent where required). Conduct analytics and improve website functionality. Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only disclose personal information to: Trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and services (e.g., web hosting, analytics providers, email services, and text messaging platforms) under strict confidentiality agreements. These providers are contractually prohibited from using your data for any other purpose. Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights.

Mobile messaging disclaimers:

  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except for aggregators and providers of the Text Message services that are contractually obligated to use this data solely to facilitate our messaging program.
  • All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the Text Message services.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for functionality, performance, and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Third-party tools (e.g., Google Analytics) may collect anonymized data; please review their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures (including encryption where appropriate) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to opt out of certain uses. To exercise these rights or for any privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@i-nett.com. For EU residents, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For California residents, we comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and applicable regulations.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on our website. Your continued use of the site or services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at service@i-nett.com.
