Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to take all four pillars?

No. Each pillar stands on its own, and every plan is month-to-month after your initial term, so you add what the business needs when it needs it.

What does month-to-month actually mean?

After a short initial term, usually 3 to 6 months, you can leave any month. If we are not doing our job, stop paying us.

Where do you provide service?

Our teams are in San Diego, Ventura, Phoenix and Las Vegas, with national reach through the Technology Assurance Group alliance across 158 marketplaces in the US and Canada.

Can you work alongside our internal IT staff?

Yes. Many of our clients have internal IT, and we cover the specialist work, the 24/7/365 monitoring and the strategy alongside them.

How is your cyber insurance different from a standard policy?

Our partner writes cyber insurance and nothing else, placed with Lloyd's of London, so the firms we manage can qualify for stronger coverage with fewer escape clauses.
