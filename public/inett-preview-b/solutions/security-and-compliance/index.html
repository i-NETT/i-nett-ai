    Frequently asked questions

    How is your cyber insurance different from a standard policy?

    Our partner writes cyber insurance and nothing else, placed with Lloyd's of London. Because our managed security is rated highly for reducing risk, the firms we manage can qualify for stronger coverage with fewer of the escape clauses that leave businesses exposed at claim time.

    Which compliance frameworks do you cover?

    HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOC 2, NIST, CMMC and CCPA, plus industry-specific requirements. We map the framework to the controls you actually run so proof is available on demand.

    How fast do you respond to a security incident?

    Monitoring runs 24/7/365. Account takeovers are contained automatically within seconds of detection, and anything that escalates reaches an engineer who knows your environment within minutes.

    We have never had a breach. Do we need all of this?

    Most businesses put comprehensive protection in place after their first incident, which is the expensive order. Prevention costs a fraction of response, and the layers are sized to your actual risk profile.

    How do you explain security to non-technical leadership?

    In terms of risk, impact and competitive position rather than tooling. Quarterly reviews show your posture in plain language with the decisions attached.
