Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How is your cybersecurity insurance different from standard policies?

Most cybersecurity insurance policies contain escape clauses that can leave businesses unprotected when they need coverage most. Because i-NETT's managed security is rated highly for reducing risk by our Lloyd's of London cyber insurance partner, the firms we manage can qualify for stronger cyber coverage with fewer common escape clauses, often at discounted pricing.

What compliance regulations can you help us address?

Our compliance management services cover major regulatory frameworks including HIPAA for healthcare, PCI DSS for payment processing, SOC 2 for service organizations, NIST frameworks, ISO 27001, and industry-specific regulations. We tailor our approach to your specific compliance needs.

How quickly can you respond to security incidents?

Our 24/7 security operations center monitors your systems continuously. Critical security incidents receive immediate attention with rapid response protocols initiated within minutes of detection.

Do we need all these security services if we've never had a breach?

The majority of businesses experience a security incident before implementing comprehensive protection. Our approach is to prevent breaches before they occur, which is significantly less costly and disruptive than responding after a breach has happened. Our services are tailored to your specific risk profile.

How do you make security understandable for non-technical leaders?

We translate technical security concepts into business terms, focusing on risk, impact, and competitive advantage rather than technical specifications. Our quarterly business reviews provide clear, executive-friendly insights into your security posture.
