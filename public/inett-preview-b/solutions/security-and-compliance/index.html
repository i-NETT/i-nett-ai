The scale of it

Why enterprise-grade tooling is not optional any more

Four numbers explain why the commercial-grade tools most businesses run cannot keep up, and what changes when an enterprise platform is doing the filtering.

1,968 cyberattacks per organization, every week

Why commercial-grade tooling cannot keep pace

  • This volume is machine-generated. Human-only triage was never going to match it
  • AI has collapsed the cost of running attacks at scale, so the volume keeps climbing
  • Detection has to be automated before anyone can prioritise it
  • Smaller organizations are targeted precisely because their tooling is lighter
4,330 security alerts a typical organization generates per day

Enterprise platforms correlate, commercial tools only alert

  • Correlation collapses thousands of raw signals into a handful of real incidents
  • Without it, the one alert that matters is buried in the noise around it
  • Email, firewall, endpoint and identity are read together, not separately
  • Your team should be handed conclusions, not a raw feed
37% of those alerts actually get investigated

The other 63% is not safe, it is simply unexamined

  • Enterprise tooling auto-closes the provably benign and records why
  • Analyst attention goes to what is genuinely ambiguous
  • Nothing is dismissed silently, there is an audit trail either way
  • Coverage stops depending on who happened to be looking that day
7 in-house IT staff expected to absorb all of it

No team of seven covers 24/7/365 on its own

  • Attacks do not wait for business hours, and neither can monitoring
  • The platform vendor's own specialists are on watch overnight and at weekends
  • Your in-house people work on the business instead of the queue
  • When something escalates it reaches someone who knows your environment

Figures are third-party industry research, not i-NETT measurements: Check Point 2026 Cyber Security Report · Ponemon Institute, 2026 State of SecOps.

Because i-NETT's managed security is rated highly for reducing risk by our Lloyd's of London cyber insurance partner, the firms we manage can qualify for stronger cyber coverage with fewer common escape clauses, often at discounted pricing.

What compliance regulations can you help us address?

Our compliance management services cover major regulatory frameworks including HIPAA for healthcare, PCI DSS for payment processing, SOC 2 for service organizations, NIST frameworks, ISO 27001, and industry-specific regulations. We tailor our approach to your specific compliance needs.

How quickly can you respond to security incidents?

Our 24/7 security operations center monitors your systems continuously. Critical security incidents receive immediate attention with rapid response protocols initiated within minutes of detection.

Do we need all these security services if we've never had a breach?

The majority of businesses experience a security incident before implementing comprehensive protection. Our approach is to prevent breaches before they occur, which is significantly less costly and disruptive than responding after a breach has happened.
