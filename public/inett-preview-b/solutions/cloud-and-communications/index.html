Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to move everything to the cloud?

No. We support hybrid environments and phased migrations sized to how your business operates.

What makes your cloud offering different from other providers?

We lead with the business plan, not a spec sheet, and one team is accountable for Azure, Microsoft 365, voice and backup, backed by a Lloyd's of London cyber insurance partnership.

Does the phone system work for remote and hybrid teams?

Yes. Desk phone, desktop app or mobile, with encrypted calls, video meetings and messaging on one platform.

How do you keep cloud environments compliant?

Controls for HIPAA, PCI and other frameworks are built in, and we review the environment on a schedule so what you run matches what your policy says.

How fast can we be back after an outage?

That should be a number, not a hope, so restores are tested on a schedule and the recovery time is known before an incident.
