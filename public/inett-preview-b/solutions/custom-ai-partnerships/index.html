Honest FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will this AI replace our staff?

No. It takes the repetitive work so your skilled people spend their time on judgment and strategy.

Is there an easy button or an out-of-the-box solution?

No, and anyone selling one is selling a fantasy. AI adoption is a leadership decision, not a software install, which is why we build it with you.

How do you ensure accuracy?

Every consequential output gets human review, and critical workflows carry layered verification so no automated action bypasses your team.

What about data privacy?

We only deploy enterprise platforms with contractual guarantees that your data is not shared or used for training, and Fortify AI retains none of it across any model.

What does it cost?

$75 per user per month, all in, with one included agent per 25 employees. Custom agent builds are quoted as a fixed fee before work begins.

How long until we see ROI?

A full custom build typically takes 3 to 6 months, though many clients see early automation wins within the first 30 to 60 days.
