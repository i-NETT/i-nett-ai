Questions leadership asks

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from standard IT support?

IT support keeps existing systems running; this is the strategic layer that aligns technology investments with business objectives, from surveillance and telecom to partner buying power.

How do you make sure technology investments deliver value?

We start with the business objective, tie each investment to a measurable outcome, and review progress in quarterly strategy reviews so the roadmap adjusts as the business changes.

Do we need to replace all our current systems?

No. We get the most out of what you already own and phase in new solutions only where they deliver the greatest impact, within your budget and operating reality.

How do you stay current with new technology?

Through our alliance across 158 marketplaces we see which technologies deliver real business value across many industries, and which ones only generate hype.

Can you work with our existing IT team?

Yes. Many of our clients have internal IT, and we add specialist expertise, strategic guidance and supplemental support alongside them.
