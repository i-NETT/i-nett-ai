FAQs About Managed IT Services in San Diego

What do managed IT services include?

Managed IT services typically include remote monitoring, help desk support, cybersecurity protection, patch management, backups, cloud support, communication systems support, and long-term technology guidance. The goal is to prevent problems, not just react to them.

How does outsourced IT support help small businesses?

Outsourced IT support gives small businesses access to a full team for a predictable monthly cost. It reduces reliance on one internal generalist, improves response times, and helps leadership stay focused on the business instead of recurring IT issues.

What is the difference between IT support and managed IT services?

IT support is usually reactive. Something breaks, and you call for help. Managed IT services are proactive. Your provider monitors systems, helps prevent issues, strengthens security, and supports long-term planning.

Does i-NETT serve businesses across San Diego County?

Yes. i-NETT provides both remote and on-site IT support for businesses across San Diego County, depending on the issue and what will resolve it most effectively.

Can managed IT services improve cybersecurity?

Yes. Managed IT services can strengthen cybersecurity through ongoing monitoring, endpoint protection, email security, patching, user support, and compliance guidance. i-NETT is also a Lloyd's of London cyber insurance partner, which can help the businesses we manage qualify for stronger cyber coverage.

Do you support cloud and communications systems?

Yes. i-NETT supports cloud environments, cloud migrations, VoIP systems, and unified communications for businesses that need stable access, collaboration, and operational flexibility.

How do I know if my business needs a managed service provider?

Frequent downtime, recurring support issues, weak cybersecurity, poor IT planning, and leadership time spent solving technology problems are all signs that your business may need a managed service provider.
