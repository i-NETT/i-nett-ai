Our Story: Evolution and Revolution

For over two decades, i-NETT has consistently challenged industry conventions to better serve our clients. Our history is one of constant evolution and intentional revolution.

From our early days to our current position as part of a billion-dollar technology organization, we've remained focused on one goal: helping growth-focused businesses leverage technology to increase profitability and outpace competition.
