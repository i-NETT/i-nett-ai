The Problem With Traditional Cybersecurity Insurance

Standard cybersecurity insurance policies often create a false sense of security through complex exclusions and technical requirements that, if not met, void coverage precisely when you need it most.

Common Insurance Gaps

Technicality-Based Denials

Most policies contain technical requirements that, if not perfectly implemented, void coverage during an incident

Security Implementation Gaps

Policies may require specific security measures without providing assistance in implementing them

Delayed Response Coverage

Many policies don't cover immediate incident response costs, leaving you scrambling during a critical time

Limited Protection Scope

Standard policies often exclude key areas like regulatory fines, legal defense costs, or business interruption

Difficult Claims Process

Complex and lengthy claims processes delay critical recovery assistance

No Proactive Coverage

Traditional policies typically only activate after an incident, with no proactive protection

When a cyber incident occurs, the last thing you need is to discover your policy contains exclusions that leave you unprotected. Unfortunately, this scenario is all too common with standard policies.

  • Comprehensive Coverage: Protection for the full spectrum of cyber threats, including ransomware, data breaches, business interruption, and regulatory fines

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the cyber insurance advantage different from standard cyber insurance?

Coverage available through our Lloyd's of London partnership has fewer of the common escape clauses found in typical policies that often void coverage during an incident. Because i-NETT's managed security is rated highly for reducing risk, the firms we manage can qualify for stronger protection that is less likely to be undone by the technical factors that typically void other policies.

Will this coverage cost more than our current policy?

In most cases, our clients actually experience a reduction in premium costs due to our unique partnership and approach to risk management. During your insurance analysis, we'll provide a detailed comparison of coverage and costs.

Do we need to implement specific security measures to qualify?

While effective security measures are important, our approach focuses on realistic, business-appropriate controls rather than the rigid, often impractical requirements found in standard policies. We work with you to implement protections that make sense for your specific business context.

How does the claims process work if we experience an incident?

Unlike the complex, often adversarial claims process typical of standard policies, our approach emphasizes rapid response and recovery. We work directly with our Lloyd's of London partners to ensure claims are processed quickly and effectively, minimizing business impact.

Can this coverage adapt as our business grows and changes?

Yes. Our ongoing management approach includes regular reviews and adjustments to ensure your protection evolves with your business. This ensures you maintain comprehensive coverage regardless of how your technology environment changes.
