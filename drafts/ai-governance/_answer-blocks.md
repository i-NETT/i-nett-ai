## /fortify-ai

_Implicit question: What is Fortify AI?_

Fortify AI is the managed AI offering from i-NETT, a Southern California technology firm that deploys secure, compliance-ready AI infrastructure for mid-market businesses with 30 to 150 employees. Each deployment is configured to the customer's regulatory environment, isolated to a dedicated tenant, and operated by i-NETT's managed services team, with customer data never used for model training or shared across clients. Fortify AI is built for HIPAA, GDPR, SOC, and CCPA requirements and is underwritten by a Lloyd's of London cybersecurity insurance policy.
